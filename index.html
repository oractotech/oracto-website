  • Fast List Creation: Build a list in seconds with zero friction.
  • Quick Item Reuse: Never type the same recurring checklist items twice.
  • Custom Categories: Color-coded categories with friendly emojis.

Private & On-Device

Your data. Always in your hands.

No login. No cloud. Your lists and notes stay on your device, with simple backup and restore whenever you need it.

  • No Account Needed: Start using Relisto immediately without signing up, giving an email, or setting a password.
  • Stored on Your Device: All list items and notes are saved directly in local storage on your iPhone.
  • Local File Backup: Export your data to a backup file anytime. Save it to Files, AirDrop, or your personal drive and restore it whenever you switch devices.
