About Relisto

Relisto helps you create reusable lists and keep your notes organized, without requiring an account or cloud storage.

Save your everyday items once, reuse them whenever you need, and keep important information organized in one place.

Feedback & Bug Reports

You can submit feedback, report bugs, or request new features directly from the Relisto app using the built-in feedback form.

This is the easiest way to send us details about your experience and help us improve Relisto.

Privacy

Relisto's lists and notes are stored locally on your device. Relisto does not require an account or cloud storage for its core functionality.

For more information about how Relisto handles data, please see our Privacy Policy .
